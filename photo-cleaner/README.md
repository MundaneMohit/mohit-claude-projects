# Photo cleaner

Free up space on an Android phone without losing anything you care about.

- Copies all photos and videos to your PC first (the phone is only read).
- Finds exact and near duplicates, blurry shots, screenshots, WhatsApp forwards, big videos and documents.
- Groups faces into people, so you can protect the people you want to keep.
- Shows everything in a local review page; you decide what to delete or compress.
- Compressing big videos keeps dates and location.

Setup and use: [SETUP.md](SETUP.md). With Claude Code, just say "set up the photo cleaner".
