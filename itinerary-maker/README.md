# Itinerary maker

Turn your booking confirmations into one friendly trip page: an animated hero scene, a "to do before the trip"
list, booking links, a card per day with green (booked) and orange (still to do) chips, and a spend table.

- [template.html](template.html): a sample page for a made-up trip. Open it in a browser to see the style.
- [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md): how Claude builds a page for your trip and keeps it current.

With Claude Code or claude.ai: attach your tickets and hotel receipts and say
"make a trip page like itinerary-maker/template.html for my trip".

Your real trip page will hold booking numbers: keep it private and share it only with people on the trip.
Do not commit it to this repo.
